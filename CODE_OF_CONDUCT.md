# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Help newcomers
understand the next step. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private
information, spam, or pressure people for unpaid work.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.
Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive content, close discussions, or restrict
participation, with proportionate action and an explanation when safe to provide.
Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a sensitive project report,
request a private channel without publishing details; do not disclose another
person's private information. Maintainers should recuse themselves from reports
involving their own conduct.
